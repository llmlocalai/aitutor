description: Questions about what was actually spent, by whom, where or when, from expense records
---
Call query_expenses before stating any amount. Name the filters you used in the answer.
A follow-up question means another query. Do not reuse totals from an earlier turn.
When the result is empty, read the diagnostic, relax the filter it names, and try once more.
Never explain why spending could not exist. Say what you searched and that you found nothing.
