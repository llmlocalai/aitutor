# Northwind Remote Work Policy

Policy number NW-HR-031.

## Section 1. Definitions

The term home office stipend means a fixed monthly payment that offsets internet and equipment costs for an employee who works remotely at least three days a week.

## Section 2. Stipend

The home office stipend is 80 dollars per month. Equipment above 400 dollars needs approval from the approving official, as defined in Section 1 of policy NW-FIN-012.
