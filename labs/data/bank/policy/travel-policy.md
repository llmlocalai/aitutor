# Northwind Travel and Expense Policy

Policy number NW-FIN-012. This is the authoritative rule. Guides and memos explain it and never override it.

## Section 1. Definitions

The term per diem means a fixed daily allowance that covers meals and incidental costs while an employee travels on company business.

The term approving official means the manager who holds budget authority for the cost center charged with the trip.

An unliquidated advance means cash given to a traveler before a trip that has not yet been matched to receipts.

## Section 2. Meals

The meal limit is 75 dollars per day in standard cities and 95 dollars per day in high-cost cities. Alcohol is never reimbursable. Receipts are required for any single meal above 25 dollars.

## Section 3. Lodging

Lodging is reimbursed at actual cost up to 220 dollars per night. A stay above the cap needs written approval from the approving official before the trip. See Section 5 for the approval steps.

## Section 4. Air travel

Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or more. Tickets must be booked at least 14 days before departure unless the approving official records a business reason.

## Section 5. Approvals

A trip that will cost more than 2,500 dollars in total needs approval before any booking. The approving official must record the decision in the expense system. An advance may not exceed 60 percent of the approved trip estimate.

## Section 6. Deadlines

Expense reports are due within 30 days after the trip ends. A report filed after 60 days is not reimbursed without a waiver from the finance director. An unliquidated advance older than 45 days is deducted from payroll.
