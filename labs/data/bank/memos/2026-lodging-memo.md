# Memo: Lodging cap reminder for conference season

Finance has seen several hotel stays above the cap. The cap is 220 dollars per night and it applies to every city. Ask your approving official in writing before you book a more expensive room. Commentary only. See Section 3 of policy NW-FIN-012.

## Common questions

Can I split a room cost across two nights to stay under the cap? No. The cap applies to the nightly rate.

Does the cap include taxes? Yes. The cap is the total nightly charge.
