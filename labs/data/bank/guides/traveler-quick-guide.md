# Traveler Quick Guide

A plain-language summary of policy NW-FIN-012. If this guide and the policy disagree, the policy wins.

## Before you go

Get approval first when the whole trip will cost more than 2,500 dollars. Book flights two weeks ahead. You can ask for an advance, and it is capped at 60 percent of the estimate.

## While you travel

Keep receipts for meals over 25 dollars. Most cities allow 75 dollars a day for food. A few expensive cities allow 95 dollars.

The meal limit is 75 dollars per day in standard cities and 95 dollars per day in high-cost cities. Alcohol is never reimbursable. Receipts are required for any single meal above 25 dollars.

## When you return

File your report within 30 days. After 60 days you need a waiver. See Section 6 of the policy.
